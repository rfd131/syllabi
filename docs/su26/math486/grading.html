How Your Grade is Determined

Grading Overview

MATH 486 uses a traditional points-based grading scheme. Your final grade is determined by the total points you earn out of a 400-point course total, with up to 20 points of extra credit available through participation in interactive games and other opportunities announced during the term.

Homework Revisions and Exceptions

Late homework

48 hours past the due date for 80% credit.

Homework revisions

Following grading of each Written Homework, you may submit a Homework Revision (guidelines posted on Canvas). A revision can earn back up to 50% of any points missed on the assignment. If the original was submitted late, the revised score is also subject to the 80% late cap.

Exceptions (two per semester)

You have two exceptions per semester. Using an exception lets you submit a Written Homework as a revision and still earn full credit for the assignment. To use an exception, notify the instructor (via the Google Form in Canvas) before the due date passes, then submit the completed homework as a revision by the revision due date. Indicate use of the exception on your cover page.

Be mindful of the relative importance of homework. Homework accounts for 200 of the 400 total course points. Consistent work on homework, quizzes, and participation throughout the course is the best way to support your learning.
