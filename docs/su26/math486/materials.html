MATH 486: Mathematical Theory of Games - Summer 2026

Course Materials

Course Materials

Required Textbook

Game Theory in Action by Steve Schecter and Herbert Gintis. 2016, Princeton University Press. ISBN: 978-1400880881.

The textbook is available chapter by chapter through the Penn State Library and through the Penn State Bookstore and online sellers.

Optional Textbooks & Additional Resources

Material and examples may also be drawn from:

  • Game Theory Evolving: A Problem-Centered Introduction to Modeling Strategic Interaction by Herbert Gintis (2nd ed.). 2009, Princeton University Press. ISBN: 978-0691140513.
  • Twenty Lectures on Algorithmic Game Theory by Tim Roughgarden. Cambridge University Press. ISBN: 978-1-316-62479-1.
  • Game Theory by Drew Fudenberg and Jean Tirole. 1991, MIT Press. ISBN: 978-0262061414. Available via the Penn State Library.
  • Essentials of Game Theory: A Concise Multidisciplinary Introduction by Kevin Leyton-Brown and Yoav Shoham. 2008, Morgan & Claypool. ISBN: 978-1598295931. Available via the Penn State Library.
  • A Course in Game Theory by Martin J. Osborne and Ariel Rubinstein. 1994, MIT Press. ISBN: 978-0262650403.
  • Evolutionary Games and Population Dynamics by Josef Hofbauer and Karl Sigmund. 1998, Cambridge University Press. ISBN: 978-0521625708. Available via the Penn State Library.
  • Game Theory: Analysis of Conflict by Roger B. Myerson. 1991, Harvard University Press. ISBN: 978-0674341166. Graduate-level textbook. Available via the Penn State Library.
