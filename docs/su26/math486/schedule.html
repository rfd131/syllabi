MATH 486: Mathematical Theory of Games - Summer 2026

Schedule of Topics

Schedule of Topics

The course outline follows the textbook, Game Theory in Action by Steve Schecter and Herbert Gintis. We have 10 lessons, one midterm, and a final spread over 12 weeks (May 18 – Aug 12, 2026).

Lessons 1–7: Non-cooperative Games

Lesson Week Topics Readings
Lesson 1 Week 1 Extensive form games; Information in extensive form games; Normal form games; Backward induction; Threats and commitments Sections 1.1–1.8 and lecture notes
Lesson 2 Week 2 Continuous strategy games; Stackelberg duopoly; Dominated strategies; Prisoner's dilemma; Best responses Sections 1.9–1.13 and 2.1–2.4
Lesson 3 Week 3 Dominant and dominated strategies; Auctions and weakly dominated strategies; Iterated elimination of dominated strategies; Backward induction and iterated elimination Sections 2.5–2.13
Lesson 4 Week 4 Nash equilibrium; Applications of Nash equilibrium; Cournot duopoly Sections 3.1–3.11
Lesson 5 Week 5 Expected payoffs; Extensive form games with incomplete information; Mixed strategies Sections 4.1–4.6 and 5.1–5.2
Lesson 6 Week 6 Mixed strategy Nash equilibria; Nash's Existence Theorem; Fundamental Theorem of Nash equilibria Sections 5.1–5.11
Lesson 7 Week 8 Subgame perfect equilibria; Repeated games; Strategies in the Repeated prisoners' dilemma game; The Folk Theorem; Bargaining Sections 6.1–6.11

Lessons 8–10: Evolutionary Games

Lesson Week Topics Readings
Lesson 8 Week 9 Introduction to evolutionary games; Evolutionary stability; Applications Sections 8.1, 8.3–8.5
Lesson 9 Week 10 Autonomous Differential equations; Phase lines and equilibria; Vectorfields; Stability; Linear systems of Differential equations; Linearization of nonlinear systems of differential equations; Phase plane analysis Sections 9.1–9.5
Lesson 10 Week 11 The Replicator dynamic; Evolutionary dynamics for two and three strategy games; Phase plane analysis and evolutionary dynamics Sections 10.1–10.11
